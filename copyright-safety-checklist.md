# Copyright & Trademark Safety Checklist

Before publishing, ensure:

- [ ] All images are either original, licensed, or from Unsplash (see `image-sources.md`).
- [ ] No images contain visible third-party logos, trademarks, or recognizable people without consent.
- [ ] All placeholder marketing copy labeled `[REPLACE]` has been replaced with original, approved content.
- [ ] Font usage complies with open-source licenses (Inter, Space Grotesk, Source Sans Pro).
- [ ] No company names, slogans, or phrases that could be trademarked are present.
- [ ] SVG wordmark is unique and not derived from any existing logo.
- [ ] All design tokens and color palettes are original or generic.
