# Image Sources & Replacement Guidelines

## Current Usage
- All images are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com).
- Search terms used: "minimalist lamp", "ceramic mug", "desk organizer".

## Replacement Guidelines
- Only use images you have rights to (Unsplash, your own, or licensed stock).
- Replace Unsplash images with your own product photos before launch.
- Ensure alt text is descriptive and accurate.
- Do not use images with visible trademarks, logos, or recognizable people without permission.
